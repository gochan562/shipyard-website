SHIPYARD.

A Discord community for high schoolers turning ideas into real, used software. Any tools welcome.

Get Discord invite → See the principles ↗

What this is.

The Structure A Discord server. That's it.
The Cost Free. Always.

A Discord server for students building real things. No curriculum, no tutorials, no certificates.

"If a real person on the internet can't use what you built, we haven't done our job."

AI coding agents, raw Rust, visual tools, Bash scripts. All count. You just need to want to ship something real.

The Principles.

01
Freedom.

Pick your tools. AI agents, raw Python, no-code. We don't grade your stack.

  • Use whatever gets you to release.
  • No tool is "less than" here.
02
Momentum.

We ship. Tutorial purgatory is the default elsewhere.

  • Progress toward actual releases.
  • Stop building when it works.
03
Ownership.

If your name's on it, the bugs are yours. No adult bailouts.

  • Test what you ship.
  • Credit every collaborator.

Where we hang out.

Shipyard is a Discord server. No portal, no dashboard. You join, pick channels, and start building.

#welcome Introductions and community mission.
#announcements Official news and events.
#help Stuck? Post here. Async help.
#ships Show what you're working on.
#working-on Get feedback on unfinished projects.
#free-space Find teammates, brainstorm, and anything else.
#events Announce member-hosted events.
#ai Cursor, Claude Code, v0. Tips and workflows.

Questions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Start with zero experience. Ship anyway with AI tools and peer help.

Do I have to use AI coding tools?

No. Most do, but raw-code members ship regularly. Freedom of tools is core here.

Who runs Shipyard?

A council of students. Adults handle legal and safety. That's it.

Is it actually free?

Yes. You keep 100% ownership of your work.

Can I host my own workshop?

Yes. Post in #events, see who shows up. You don't need permission. Just post it.
